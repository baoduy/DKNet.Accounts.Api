Three of the theme's action tokens need an accessible companion. As text on the page ground the lime measures 1.30:1, so --link carries links — the slate in light, the lime in dark. --ring is the lime too, at 1.34:1 against a card, so focus is drawn with --focus-ring. And --accent (#f0fdf4) is lighter than --muted (#f1f5f9), which made a selected row read paler than a hovered one — selection uses --surface-selected, a wash of the primary's own hue.
