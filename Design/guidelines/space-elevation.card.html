Two semantic names alias into the scale so component code never picks a step by number: --shadow-card is sm (every card and table), --shadow-overlay is xl (dialogs and the detail panel only). The wide low-opacity spread is what makes a white card read as lifted off the off-white ground rather than pasted onto it.
