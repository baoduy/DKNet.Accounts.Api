The raw tint pill. For any account, group, posting or currency status, reach for `StatusBadge` instead — it owns the one colour map.

```jsx
<Badge tone="credit">Active</Badge>
<Badge tone="warning">Dormant</Badge>
```

12px at weight 500, `2px 8px`, `radius-full`, no border. Tones: credit (emerald) · warning (orange) · debit (rose) · info (green) · neutral (grey). A pill shape is allowed here and banned on a button — the filled tint and the absence of hover already say this is not a control.
